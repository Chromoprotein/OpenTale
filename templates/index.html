Write Your Book with AI Assistance

This tool guides you through the process of writing a book with AI help. Follow these steps:

  1. Create Story Synopsis
    Start with a synopsis to guide your story
  2. Create World Setting
    Define the setting, time period, and environment for your story
  3. Develop Characters
    Create the characters that will populate your story world
  4. Generate Book Outline
    Create a chapter-by-chapter outline of your story
  5. Write Action Beats and Chapters
    Generate and edit each chapter of your book

About This Tool

This is an open-source tool that uses AI to help you write a book. All content is generated locally on your computer and saved in the book_output folder. You can edit, modify, and export your book at any time.
